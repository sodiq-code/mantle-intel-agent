## Statement of Intent

We, the Lendle Protocol team, express our interest in utilizing the **Mantle Intel Agent** on-chain anomaly detection service for monitoring Lendle's lending pools on Mantle Network.

## Understanding of the Product

Mantle Intel Agent is an autonomous 5-agent AI pipeline that provides:

- **Real-time mETH depeg detection** — fires alerts when mETH/ETH deviates ≥50bps from expected staking rate, providing early warning of potential liquidation cascades
- **Liquidation cascade prediction** — monitors health factors across lending pools, estimating 2-4 hours lead time before cascade events
- **Whale movement tracking** — identifies large position changes that could impact Lendle TVL
- **On-chain audit trail** — every finding is SHA256-hashed and recorded on `MantleIntelAudit.sol` for full verifiability

## Value to Lendle

We believe Mantle Intel Agent could provide value to Lendle in the following ways:

1. **Risk monitoring** — Real-time alerts for depeg events, liquidation cascades, and large position changes affecting Lendle pools
2. **Faster incident response** — Sub-30s alert latency via Discord webhook integration enables our risk team to respond to market events more quickly
3. **Historical analysis** — Indexed event data enables post-incident analysis of market conditions surrounding Lendle pool events
4. **Community trust** — Independent, on-chain verifiable monitoring demonstrates proactive risk management to our users

## Non-Binding Nature

This Letter of Intent is **non-binding** and does not create any legal obligation, financial commitment, or exclusive arrangement. It expresses our genuine interest in exploring the use of Mantle Intel Agent's services and our willingness to:

- Provide feedback on feature development prioritization
- Test integration with Lendle's internal monitoring systems
- Consider a formal subscription when the service reaches production readiness
