- **Whale movement alerts** — detects large position changes and swaps that could impact concentrated liquidity ranges

3. **Protocol monitoring** — Continuous anomaly detection helps our team respond to unusual trading patterns, potential MEV attacks, or liquidity events
4. **User engagement** — Integrating intelligence alerts into the Agni interface would increase user engagement and retention
