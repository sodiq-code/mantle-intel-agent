## Statement of Intent

We, the Merchant Moe Protocol team, express our interest in utilizing the **Mantle Intel Agent** on-chain anomaly detection service for monitoring Merchant Moe's liquidity pools and DEX activity on Mantle Network.

## Understanding of the Product

Mantle Intel Agent is an autonomous 5-agent AI pipeline that provides:

- **Liquidity imbalance detection** — fires alerts when AMM reserve ratios shift ≥15% from baseline, indicating potential impermanent loss risk for LPs
- **Whale accumulation/distribution tracking** — identifies large swaps and position changes that could impact Merchant Moe pool pricing
- **Cross-protocol correlation** — detects simultaneous activity across multiple Mantle DeFi protocols, which often precedes significant price movements
- **On-chain audit trail** — every finding is SHA256-hashed and recorded on `MantleIntelAudit.sol` for full verifiability

## Value to Merchant Moe

We believe Mantle Intel Agent could provide value to Merchant Moe in the following ways:

1. **LP protection** — Real-time reserve imbalance alerts help LPs adjust positions before significant impermanent loss occurs
2. **Whale activity awareness** — Tracking large swaps through Merchant Moe pools gives our team and users visibility into informed flow vs noise
3. **Protocol health monitoring** — Continuous monitoring of pool reserves, volume, and TVL trends helps identify anomalous patterns early
4. **User experience** — Integrating real-time alerts into the Merchant Moe interface would provide a differentiated user experience vs other Mantle DEXs

## Non-Binding Nature
